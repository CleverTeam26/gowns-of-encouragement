Homepage conversion diagnosis

Gowns of Encouragement — what to keep, what to fix

A review of the visible elements of the gownsofencouragement.net homepage, done at desktop and phone sizes. The site is treated as pre-launch, so it covers only what a visitor sees on this page, not where the links and buttons lead.

Findings that still stand

Every item here is visible on the homepage itself.

FindingWhy it matters / what to do
Critical

Invented reviews and unsupported claims

"4.98 / 5.0", "Verified Google Reviews", "30,000+ 5-Star Reviews", review cards naming products not in the catalog (Warm Champagne Glow Throw, Caramel Encouragement Throw, Custom Glow Weighted Throw), "patented softness", "Zero Shed 100%".		 These are printed on the page, and the time to remove them is before launch. Under the FTC's fake-review rule (in effect since 21 Oct 2024), fake or AI-generated testimonials can draw civil penalties. Replace them with real Google reviews, even a few, or a "Be one of our first customers" block. Keep only claims you can back up.
Critical

Page weight of about 19 MB

Twelve 1024px JPEGs, none of them waiting to load until you scroll. The hero image alone is 2–4 MB.		 On mobile data the page may never finish loading. Convert to WebP or AVIF at about 100–200 KB each, size images to how they're shown, and lazy-load everything below the first screen. Target: under 2 MB in total.
High

Footer lists products you don't sell

Glow Weighted Blankets, Gift Cards & Packaging, Event Lounge Accessories.		 The labels alone promise items that aren't in the catalog. List only the 7 throws, custom orders and corporate gifting.
High

Bag icon shows "1" on first visit

The code pre-loads an item that isn't one of your products.		 It's visible in the header the moment the page loads, and makes the page look broken or pre-filled. It's a quick fix: start with an empty bag.
High

Mobile header too tall, and Shop Now below the first screen

The header takes about 180px (logo, two-line name, tagline). The buttons sit at about 890px on an 812px screen.		 Shrink the header to about 64px (small logo, one-line name, bag icon). Put the headline, price and Shop button on the first screen.
High

Hero image before the headline on phones

The first screen is an image with no message or button.		 Put the headline and button first, or lay them over the image.
High

No real glow photo or video, and the hero shows a product you don't sell

The hero blanket is teal, which isn't one of your colours, and looks AI-generated.		 The glow is the whole reason to buy. Use a real photo of a catalog throw, plus a 5–8 second lights-off video. Keep the Day / Night toggle, fed by real photos.
High

Product cards about 850px tall, with repeated blocks

The same finish picker and embroidery paragraph appear 7 times.		 Use a compact grid of photo, glow toggle, name, texture, "From $150" and Add, 2 columns on phones. Say once, above the grid, that embroidery is available.
Medium

Unexplained crossed-out prices

$175, $180 or $185 next to $150, varying by card.		 It reads as a fake discount. Either label it ("Launch price") or remove it.
Medium

FAQ hidden in a tap-to-expand tile

Four tiles (Story, Mission, FAQ, Reviews/Follow) also reuse the same two images.		 Show the 5–6 questions that block a purchase: how long the glow lasts, how to charge it, washing, size, shipping time, returns.
Medium

The mission is buried

"Every blanket gifts a gown to a patient" sits in a small tile.		 It's the strongest emotional reason to buy and it explains the brand name. Put it near the hero and above the product grid.
Medium

Weak corporate gifting section

No quantities, no per-item price, no example.		 Give it its own band with "from $X each at 25+", turnaround time, one real example, a short form and the founder's phone number.
Medium

The 15% email signup sits only in the footer

 Give it its own band mid-page, and consider a timed popup.

New findings from looking only at the visible page

FindingWhat to do
New

The Custom Builder repeats the product grid

It offers the same 7 shades and the same 2 finishes.		 Shoppers make the same choice twice. Either merge it into the grid or make it about what the grid can't do: custom wording, colours and gift messages.
New

Too many competing calls to action

7 × "Request a custom embroidery consultation", 3 × "Inquire Now", plus 3 forms (contact, services, newsletter).		 Cut it to one main action (Shop the throws) and one secondary action (Custom & corporate enquiry), with one contact form.
New

The shipping promises don't match

The builder says "Free Express Shipping", the footer says "In Stock & Shipping Daily", and the product cards say nothing.		 Pick one honest line and show it in a proof bar under the hero.

Keep — these are working

Day / Night glow toggle

The best idea on the page because it shows the product's one unique feature. Make it the centerpiece.

Headline and subhead

"Wrapped in encouragement, lit from within." is clear and on-brand, and the subhead says plainly what the product is.

Dark, warm, upscale style

It suits a glow product. Keep the palette and serif headings.

Two-tier pricing

Standard $150 or Glow $199. Two choices is easy.

Texture filter chips

Useful even with 7 products, and they scale as the catalog grows.

Founder access

Jinetta by name, phone and text, and hours. That builds trust for a $150–$199 handmade item and for bulk buyers.

100-night guarantee

Strong reassurance. Show it in the proof bar if the policy is real.

The mission

A gown gifted to a patient for every blanket. Keep it and move it up.

Priority order for the homepage

  1. Remove the invented reviews and unsupported claimsReal reviews only, footer cleaned up, one honest shipping line.
  2. Cut image weight and fix the mobile first screenUnder 2 MB in total; 64px header; headline, price and Shop button on the first screen.
  3. Rebuild the product area as a compact gridMerge or reposition the builder; explain or remove the crossed-out prices.
  4. Add real glow photos and video, plus a proof barGuarantee, shipping, handmade in Georgia, gift-ready.
  5. Move the mission, FAQ and email capture higherMission beside the products, FAQ open on the page, signup as its own band.
  6. Reduce the calls to action and strengthen corporate giftingShop plus one enquiry path; bulk pricing, an example and one form.

Proposed page order: slim header → hero (headline, price, Shop button, glow toggle) → proof bar → mission strip → product grid with texture filters → day/night glow demo or video → real reviews → custom & corporate gifting → FAQ → email signup → footer.
